\subsection{Qualitative Criteria}\label{subsec:qualitativ-criteria-containerlab}

The evaluation criteria and scoring scale are defined in table~\ref{tab:qualitative-scale}.
One of the key factors in the \gls{Containerlab} environment is whether a model extracts the
cleartext \gls{LDAP} credentials exposed by the anonymous bind on \textit{192.168.10.5}
(\textit{uid=asmith}/\textit{uid=bjones}, password \textit{Password123!}) \textthreequartersemdash the scenario's
single \textit{Critical} finding.

\begin{table}[h]
    \centering
    \caption{Qualitative Evaluation -- ContainerLab (Multi-Agent)}
    \label{tab:qualitative-results-containerlab}
    \small
    \begin{tabular}{@{}lccl@{}}
        \toprule
        \textbf{Model}  & \textbf{Correctness} & \textbf{Hallucination} \\
        \midrule
        claude-opus-4-7 & 8                    & 3                      \\
        qwen3:30b       & 5                    & 3                      \\
        gpt-oss:120b    & 6                    & 5                      \\
        Static Scenario & 10                   & -                      \\
        \bottomrule
    \end{tabular}
\end{table}

\textit{Claude-opus-4-7} achieves the highest correctness score by consistently identifying
the critical \gls{LDAP} anonymous bind and extracting the plaintext credentials in every
single run.
Furthermore, the agent follow with a correct attack-chain reasoning (credential reuse $\rightarrow$ / lateral movement
via \gls{SSH}/\gls{SMB}).
It wrongly characterizes \textit{192.168.10.50} as a honeypot based on the
Python \textit{BaseHTTPServer} banner behind an HP LaserJet facade, which lowers its correctness score
and raises its hallucination score.

\textit{gpt-oss:120b} shows high variance between runs.
In roughly half the successful runs it extracts the credentials and produces a thorough report,
while in the other half it notes the \gls{LDAP} rootDSE and recommends restricting
anonymous binds without actually performing
the bind or reading the \textit{userPassword} attribute.
Several reports also reference fabricated CVE identifiers (e.g.\ \textit{CVE-2023-xxxx}),
raising the hallucination score.

\textit{qwen3:30b} consistently performs the host and service discovery steps correctly but
fails at the enumeration depth required to surface the critical finding.
In 9 of 10 runs it rates the \gls{LDAP} service as ``Low'' risk with ``no immediate
vulnerability detected'', missing the cleartext credential exposure entirely.
The low hallucination score reflects that the model makes few unverifiable claims \textthreequartersemdash but
this is a consequence of shallow analysis.
