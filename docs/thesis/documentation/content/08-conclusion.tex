\section{Personal Section}\label{sec:personal-section}

\subsection{Frank}\label{subsec:peronal-section-frank}

In this project, I gained experience in enumerating a network configuration in a virtual environment with ContainerLab.
The simplicity of how a containerized environment can be deployed and reconfigured is still really impressive.
I benefited from the opportunity to implement a file server, \gls{DNS}, and a firewall and much more, and it motivated me to follow up by building a home cyber lab.
Also, I benefited greatly from using and researching different models and the Langchain framework.
So I am definitely going to extend the lab with an \gls{LLM}.
Personally, I favor using \glspl{LLM} for user implementation, primarily for reasoning and recommendations.
The most surprising fact for me was the difficulty of deploying the \gls{NSAK} autonomously in the NSLab.
I expected runs to be much more seamless in fulfilling the task.
I liked the project management tools and ceremonies, which help structure the project, but they come with an overhead and are not suited for a part-time bachelor project.
The limitation on operating with fixed iterations in GitLab did not align with our approach of 2 blocked focus weeks.
A deep respect for well-written papers, it is a challenge to write professionally and follow the \enquote{red string}
in the whole thesis.

\subsection{Lukas}\label{subsec:peronal-section-lukas}

Initially very skeptic of the actual capabilities and usefulness of \glspl{LLM},
a year ago I would not have guessed that I will write an \gls{AI} focused bachelors thesis.
This changed drastically with the rapid improvement of the models and especially the rise of \gls{AI} agents.
Suddenly, combining the newly found interest with our previous work seemed to be the logical choice,
and the implementation of the agent into \gls{NSAK} kick-started my journey into the topic.
Of course, we repeatedly made the rooki mistake of underestimated tasks and documentation.
On the other hand, I think this kind of naivety leads to walking the extra mile,
instead of playing safe and doing only the minimum.
For me the biggest challenge was to keep the focus on the goals and research questions instead of implementing \textit{just another thing} to improve the overall setup.
I second Franks statement about scientific writing~\textthreequartersemdash~It's very time intensive and difficult to keep up the motivation for achieving an overall high quality.
Working together with Frank was a pleasure and I think we did leverage the strengths and compensate the weaknesses of one another.
All in all, it felt like putting together a puzzle of all the stuff we learned at BFH and beyond.
