\subsection{Risk Assessment}\label{subsec:risk_assessment}

To methodically conduct the risk assessment the section \enquote{Risk assessment} from an online guide published by Advisera~\cite{advisera_iso27001_risk_assessment},
which describes the risk assessment process according to \gls{iso27001}, was used as a reference.

\paragraph{Risk Assessment Process:}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Risk categories and criteria: Define which dimensions and taxonomies are important for the assessment.
    \item Risk identification: Collect possible risks in a brainstorming session.
    \item Risk analysis: Assess the risks criteria on a risk matrix.
    \item Risk evaluation: Create a list of risks ordered by the resulting risk level and evaluate if they are acceptable or need treatment.
    \item Risk treatment: Describe the strategy and how to treat the risks.
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection{Risk Categories \& Criteria:}\label{subsubsec:risk-categories-and-criteria}

The table~\ref{tab:risk-categories} shows four commonly used categories, which where used to group the risks.

\begin{table}[h]
    \begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{lX}
        \toprule
        \textbf{Category} & \textbf{Description} \\
        \midrule
        Strategic Risk & Risks related to business decisions (e.g., wrong prioritization). \\
        Operational Risk & Risks related to internal processes (e.g., Scrum, \glsxtrshort{QA} process). \\
        Financial Risk & Risks related to costs and expenses (e.g., hardware costs). \\
        External Risk & Risks related to uncontrollable sources (e.g., force majeure). \\
        \bottomrule
    \end{tabularx}
    \caption{Risk categories used for the project risk assessment}\label{tab:risk-categories}
\end{table}

The criteria for analysing the risks are summarized in the table~\ref{tab:risk-criteria}

\begin{table}[h]
    \begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{lX}
        \toprule

        \midrule

        \bottomrule
    \end{tabularx}
    \caption{Risk evaluation criteria}\label{tab:risk-criteria}
\end{table}

\begin{table}[h]
    \begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{llX}
        \toprule
        \textbf{Level} & \textbf{Weight} & \textbf{Description} \\
        \midrule

        \bottomrule
    \end{tabularx}
    \caption{Risk probability scale}\label{tab:risk-probability-scale}
\end{table}

\begin{table}[h]
    \begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{llX}
        \toprule
        \textbf{Level} & \textbf{Weight} & \textbf{Description} \\
        \midrule
        Low & 1 & Minor inconveniences \\
        Medium & 2 & Delays milestones or reduces scope \\
        High & 3 & Threat to the thesis \\
        \bottomrule
    \end{tabularx}
    \caption{Risk impact scale}\label{tab:risk-impact-scale}

The following table~\ref{tab:risk-level} defines the calculation method and the resulting risk levels, used for the assessment.

\begin{table}[h]
    \begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{llX}

        \textbf{Risk Level} & \textbf{Weight} & \textbf{Calculation} \\
        \midrule
        Very Low & 2 & Low + Low \\

        High & 5 & Medium + High \\
        Critical & 6 & High + High \\

\subsubsection{Risk Analysis}\label{subsubsec:risk_analysis}

For analysing the risks a matrix was prepared, where the criteria \enquote{Impact} and \enquote{Probability} are representing the x- and y-axis respectively.
The project team then assessed the criteria for each individual risk and placed them on the matrix accordingly.
In a second step, the risks where moved if they did not fit relatively to each other.
The resulting \enquote{Risk Analysis Matrix} is illustrated in the following figure~\ref{fig:risks-analysis-matrix}.
The background color of the cells is representing the resulting risk level of the tasks.

\subsubsection{Risk Evaluation \& Treatment}\label{subsubsec:risk_treatment}

In a first step a table with the required columns was created.
Then the risks and weights for the criteria where added with an ID in the format \enquote{R-000}, to easier reference them in the thesis later on.
After sorting the table by the calculated risk level, the risk evaluation was conducted by deciding which treatment strategy described in~\ref{tab:risk-treatment-strategies} we want to apply.

\begin{table}[H]
    \centering
    \begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{lX}
        \toprule
        \textbf{Strategy} & \textbf{Description} \\
        \midrule
        \textbf{Mitigate} & Reduce the likelihood or impact of a risk. \\
        \textbf{Transfer} & Move the risk to a third party. \\
        \textbf{Accept} & Acknowledge the risk and take no further action. \\
        \textbf{Avoid} & Eliminate or circumvent the cause of the risk. \\
        \bottomrule
    \end{tabularx}
    \caption{Risk Treatment Strategies}
    \label{tab:risk-treatment-strategies}
\end{table}
